The Story

V12 is a Miami Beach original — a DJ turned artist, producer and performer who built a following the hard way: live, online, and in the streets of the 305. His high-energy reggaeton-electronic sound turns rooms into full-blown experiences — unmistakably Miami.

In 2026 he took the Grand Prize (Adult category) of Elevated Tracks 305 — Miami's first official city-anthem contest — with his single “RUMBA,” chosen out of 135 songs and thousands of public votes. The contest was co-chaired by Gloria & Emilio Estefan and judged by Jon Secada, Rudy Pérez and Rico Love, through a partnership with the University of Miami Frost School of Music.

That same run put him on the FIFA World Cup 2026 fan-festival stage in Miami — performing for an international crowd on one of the biggest platforms the city has ever hosted. Off stage, he goes live daily to the Rari's — gaming, reacting to Miami's biggest sports nights, and premiering new music before anyone else hears it.
